# /product-discovery

Use the `product-discovery` skill before `/shape-up` when deciding what to build.

**Arguments:** `$ARGUMENTS`

Separate supplied evidence from assumptions, identify outcome and risks, choose the
cheapest evidence-changing experiments, and define success/failure thresholds. Use
`templates/product-discovery.md`; never invent customer research or implementation.
Hand only sufficiently evidenced candidates to `/shape-up`.
