# Technical debt register: <repository or area>

## Scope and evidence

Record revision, inspected paths, history period, operational sources, tools run, and excluded or unavailable areas.

## Candidates

Status is `Open`, `Selected`, `Deferred`, `Rejected`, `Resolved`, or `Stale`.
Cost is `Low`, `Medium`, `High`, or `Unknown`. Name the basis for each cost, such as the file count or the change size.
Use `Unknown` when no evidence supports a cost. Rank is the row order by the priority rationale, starting at 1.
Do not assign a rank from an invented score.

| ID and owner | Rank | Status | Evidence and concrete consequence | Options and confidence | Cost, priority, and risk rationale | Completion condition and review trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Decisions and rejected candidates

Preserve stable IDs and existing decisions.
Record duplicates, safe look-alikes, deferred items, resolved evidence, and reasons.

## Selected assessments

Link selected items to technical assessment memos and their outcomes.
Discovery alone does not authorize implementation.
